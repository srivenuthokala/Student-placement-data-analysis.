Gateway Guide & Purpose

What is the Purpose of this Landing Gateway?

This gateway is the single, centralized, role-based entry point connecting university leadership, Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), faculty branch coordinators, and graduating student candidates to their dedicated operational workspaces.

1. Role-Based Access Isolation

Institutional placement operations require strict security segregation. Administrative controls (scheduling campus drives, editing cutoffs, viewing the full multi-department roster, and downloading NIRF accreditation dossiers) are restricted exclusively to verified Faculty & TPO accounts.

  • Faculty Role: Administrative governance & analytics
  • Student Role: Personal career & eligibility tracker

2. Zero-Friction Student Access

Enrolled students do not need manual account registration. Student candidate records are pre-linked to official university Roll Numbers. Candidates log in instantly to verify real-time placement eligibility, monitor corporate interview rounds, and review verified salary packages.

  • Pre-synchronized with university enrollment rosters
  • Instant authentication via unified login gateway

3. Quick Evaluation & Inspection

For institutional auditors, recruiters, and platform evaluators, unified login allows seamless inspection of all 13 placement management features without manual database configuration.

  • Unified auto-routing login for both roles
  • Live SQLite & MySQL dual-engine connectivity

Truthful Placement Calculation

Traditional reports often inflate statistics by counting duplicate offers. EduPlace strictly calculates:
Placement Rate (%) = (Unique Placed / Eligible Denominator) × 100 Multiple offers received by high-achieving candidates are preserved in a separate disambiguated offer ledger.

Process Governance

The End-to-End Campus Recruitment Lifecycle

How EduPlace manages data from initial student ingestion to final NIRF accreditation reporting.

Step 01

Data Ingestion

Rosters ingested via Excel/CSV or single student entry with CGPA, department, and lateral-entry diploma qualifications.

Step 02

Dynamic Cutoffs

Cutoff policy is established (e.g. CGPA ≥ 6.5, 0 backlogs). The system computes the truthful eligible candidate denominator.

Step 03

Drive Scheduling

Recruiting companies post campus drives with job descriptions, CTC packages, dates, and eligible branch criteria.

Step 04

Interview Pipeline

Candidates progress through 5 interview rounds: Aptitude → Coding → Tech 1 → Tech 2 → HR Round.

Step 05

Offer Verification

Verified corporate offers are recorded with LPA packages, accepted statuses, and automatic unique student deduplication.

Step 06

Accreditation Reporting

Executive charts and audit-ready data dossiers are generated for NIRF, NAAC, NBA, and institutional stakeholders.

Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers regarding platform calculations, data ingestion, student privacy, and institutional compliance.

When you enter your credentials on the login page, the system automatically detects whether you are a Faculty member or an enrolled Student candidate. Faculty members are routed directly to the administrative Command Center, and students are routed to their personal placement dashboard.

Unlike legacy spreadsheets that divide by arbitrary counts, EduPlace uses the audited formula: Placement Rate (%) = (Unique Placed Eligible Students / Total Eligible Cohort Denominator) × 100. Ineligible students (e.g. active backlogs or CGPA below cutoff) are partitioned out of the denominator to maintain rigorous mathematical integrity.

Multiple offers are recorded in full in the Verified Placement Ledger, but the system deduplicates the student count when calculating placement rates. A candidate holding offers from Google, Microsoft, and Amazon counts as exactly ONE placed student in the numerator, avoiding artificial metric inflation.

When a Placement Officer adjusts the CGPA cutoff (e.g. from 6.0 to 6.5) or updates backlog rules, the platform executes an instant batch SQL query that updates the is_eligible flag across the entire database, immediately refreshing KPI denominators and student radar cards.

All student record modifications, offer additions, and cutoff adjustments are permanently recorded in the immutable Audit Logs table with timestamps and operator identity. Complete aggregated text dossiers and publication-ready charts can be exported with a single click.
